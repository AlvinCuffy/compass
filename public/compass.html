Morning

  1. Read the prayer
    ACTS: Adoration · Thanksgiving · Confession · Supplication

    Adoration

    God, You are good, You are faithful, and You are the one who sees me fully — thank You for who You are before I ask for anything.

    Thanksgiving

    Thank You for salvation and Your presence with me. Thank You for my health and strength this morning. Thank You for peace of mind where I have it. Thank You for Rashida, my kids, and the people You've placed around me to build with. Thank You for the ability to provide and for what this business can become.

    Confession

    Lord, I bring You what I'm still carrying — the pull toward filling idle time with old habits, and the way I protect myself by leaving instead of staying present in hard moments. I don't want to keep managing these on my own. Search me and show me where I'm still choosing the old pattern.

    Supplication

    What You think, let me think. What You want, let me want. Bless my heart with what my heart needs, my hands with what my hands need, my head with what my head needs. Put me in the right place at the right time, with the right people, saying the right words. Let me work for You today — do what pleases You, what I'm gifted at, what serves people, and what provides for my family. And where I'm tempted to fill empty time or walk away instead of staying, give me the strength to choose differently, today.
